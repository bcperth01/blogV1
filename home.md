"### What this site is for..
This site comprises a collection of articles about the changing world of software development --- with new frameworks and on-line services (IaaS and SaaS) appearing every day, not to mention AI tools.

Read the About page for a bit more depth on background and motivation.

I have allowed a door for others to contribute articles if they want.

### How to access the articles

1. Anyone can read published articles  
   Click on ""Articles"" and you can see all the articles that people have published
2. Click on ""More Details"" to read a specific article
3. You can filter articles by tags
4. You can do a full text search to find articles containing search words or phrases

### How to enter and publish an article

1. If you don't have a login, sign up and wait for your registration to be approved
2. Login in
3. Go to Articles, then press 'New Article""
4. Enter a Title and Description in plain English
5. Enter your articles in the ""Markdown"" box. As you enter markdown, a preview is displayed in the right hand pane.  
   See **notes** below if you prefer to copy and paste from vscode or any other editor that supports markdown.
6. Press ""Save"" at any time to save your article
7. When you articles is ready you can press ""Publish"" so others can read it.  
    I will review any new articles before publishing.
8. If you later change your mind you can ""Unpublish"" or ""Delete"" the article, or you can ""edit"" it at any time.

#### Notes:

- [Markdown Quick Reference Cheat Sheet](https://wordpress.com/support/markdown-quick-reference/)
- If you have a pre-existing article in markdown, you can copy-paste it into the Markdown pane and then press Save.

### Rules

1. Bad behaviour may result in your login being revoked
2. Only cerebral articles will be published
3. I may set a rate limit or quotas if anyone (or bot) is taking the proverbial P

### Who writes and owns the articles

You have to be an approved user with a login before you can enter an article.  
The articles belong to whoever entered them.

### Commercialisation

There never will be ads on this site and there is no motivation or intention ever to commercialise the site in any way.  
I have no interest in collecting peoples emails."
